Choose your map picture

Use a screenshot of your yard from Google Maps or Apple Maps (satellite view works best), or upload your own picture. This app cannot pull the map itself.

Privacy: this app does not store your address. Your picture may still show a house number or street label, and the picture is saved inside your file. Crop it first if you don't want that.

Choose how to get your yard picture.

Crop to your yard

Drag a box around your property. Cropping removes the address bar, street labels, and most of the surroundings from the saved picture.

Print

Overview shows the yard map with a legend. Zone detail adds one sheet per zone with its heads and equipment.
